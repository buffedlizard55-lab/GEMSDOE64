What must happen before “download”

Six hard gates. No exceptions.

When a gate fails or its evidence is unavailable, the status stays red. A public webpage or synthetic raster cannot substitute for the missing artifact.

  • 01Data and provenance: authorized competition files, exact shared feature cache and tools, with input hashes and grid metadata recorded.
  • 02Leakage control: feature-alone canaries; AUC above 0.90 is a leakage warning pending explanation; whole fault segments are withheld with a buffer.
  • 03Holdout lift: visible-only catalogue features, pixel-exact visible-fault mask, pooled holdout DTI, positive count and 95% CI; promotion requires over 2 block-bootstrap standard errors versus incumbent on the same surfaces.
  • 04Novelty and non-duplication: compare every registry raster before placement and final dots; stop if rank correlation exceeds 0.90 or more than 70% of dots lie within 3 pixels.
  • 05Exact output checks: template writer plus validator, matching single-band float32 grid and valid values, pixel agreement, and SHA-256 recorded.
  • 06Submission card: unique, unused name; note no longer than 140 characters; all evidence and verdict recorded. No automatic upload or slot use.

Project brief

Mechanism first. Evidence next.

Fault prediction is an inference problem with look-alike geology, label uncertainty and spatial dependence. The research register ranks 4 distinct, publicly sourced hypotheses, labels what is and is not confirmed, and names a physical mimic for each.

4 RANKED HYPOTHESES

Geological signals

Relocated hypocenter geometry, DEM-derived drainage deflection, Sentinel-1 deformation, and geothermal surface geochemistry—each subject to coverage and novelty checks.
EXPECTED DTI

Selection, not thresholding

Choose candidates by exact mean marginal score change across plausible truth rasters. Local probabilities alone ignore the spatial redundancy in the score kernel.
SOURCE REGISTER

Open does not mean ready

Free/offical sources are linked alongside license, spatial coverage, access and retrieval caveats. No proposed layer is presented as downloaded or integrated.

Operating principles

Maximize P(Win). Own the Outcome.

Maximize P(Win): scarce experiment and submission budgets go to tests with a measurable chance of spatial holdout improvement, not leaderboard imitation.

Own the Outcome: the negative decision, missing inputs, score uncertainty, and next unblock steps are written down. Failed or blocked tests stay visible.

Leaderboard monitoring and score mirroring are intentionally excluded. See the official Terms of Use and use the competition page directly for current information, subject to its terms.
