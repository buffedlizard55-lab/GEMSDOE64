2. Why h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros (0.2778) Scored Highest Among Prior GEMSDOE Sites

Byte-level forensic analysis (scripts/audit_h33.py, evidence/h33_forensics.json) of gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif (sha256: c55bafc470054e8271dcb89347a17e07fefe50de6af6e6ba6c4b169ef7ab6fa9, 37,654 positive pixels) against its parent GEMSDOE28__h27-4-r1-solo-d2-8.tif (sha256: 2fc94a38d77f74d4f4ed1a97a83e7bb71a1ceea090515ec641e6681cc47c44c8, 40,199 positive pixels) establishes:

  1. Exact Pixel Identity with a $200\text{ m}$ Catalogue-Flank Pruning Rule (rule_pixel_disagreement = 0):
  2. h33-2-b2 is pixel-for-pixel identical to removing the 2,545 dots that lie at Euclidean distance $0 < d \le 2\text{ px}$ ($200\text{ m}$) from existing_faults.tif, while retaining all 37,654 dots at $d \ge 2.236\text{ px}$ ($\sqrt{5}\times 100\text{ m}$), and adding 0 new pixels.
  3. Mathematical Mechanism Under Distance-Tolerant IoU ($\text{DTI}, \alpha=0.2, \beta=0.8, R=300\text{ m}$):
  4. Under the competition metric: $$\text{DTI} = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{\text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w + 0.8\,\text{FN}_w}, \quad w(d) = \max!\left(0, 1 - \frac{d}{300\text{ m}}\right)$$
  5. Known catalogue fault pixels ($60,988$ cells) are masked at evaluation time (masked_known). Consequently, a predicted dot placed at $d = 100\text{--}200\text{ m}$ perpendicular to an already-mapped fault strand receives zero $\text{TP}_w$ credit from the masked strand while still incurring up to $+0.2$ in the denominator ($0.2\,\text{FP}_w$) unless an unmapped fault branch happens to sit there.
  6. Even at genuine fault tips where an unmapped continuation begins at $d = 100\text{ m}$ from the catalogue tip, a single predicted dot placed at $d = 300\text{ m}$ along strike already covers the $100\text{--}200\text{ m}$ tip cells via the $R=300\text{ m}$ triangular kernel ($w(200\text{ m})=1/3, w(100\text{ m})=2/3$), because $\text{TP}_w$ takes the maximum over nearby predictions (1 - d_to_pred / R), whereas $\text{FP}_w$ sums additively over every emitted dot!
  7. Pruning the 2,545 redundant inner-collar dots reduced $\text{FP}_w$ by $\sim 2,500$ units with negligible loss of $\text{TP}_w$, lifting the owner-reported score from 0.2708 (h27-4-r1-solo-d2-8) to 0.2778 (h33-2-b2).
  8. Why Tip-Collar Pruning Alone Plateaus and How H64-L1 Goes Beyond It:
  9. Because h33-2-b2 places 95.8% of its 37,654 dots within $2.0\text{ km}$ of already-mapped catalogue tips, it is nearly blind to whole unmapped fault segments in intrabasin valleys and piedmont fans (HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.003814, 95% CI [0.003033, 0.004641] on gems57-pooled-hide-v3-se with $N=9,275$ withheld positive pixels).
  10. Reaching and beating 0.3195 and 0.3774 requires genuine off-catalogue surface & geophysical fault discovery (native 1–3 m LiDAR scarplet template matching + multiscale Steger/Frangi Hessian line extraction + cultural-mimic masking + 20 km block-calibrated spatial CV) combined with $R=300\text{ m}$ non-maximum suppression so emitted picks do not double-count $\text{FP}_w$.

Experiment 1 (EXP-64-1): Native 3 m DEM Tile Verification (x40y427_3m.tif & x42y425_3m.tif)

  • Implementation: src/gems64/lidar_scarp.py (scarplet_template_kernel, compute_native_scarplet_steger_frangi_tile, aggregate_tile_to_100m) and scripts/run_lidar_scarp_lane.py (evidence/exp1_native_tile_verification.json).
  • Formulation:
  • Slope-Detrended Surface: $z_{\text{det}} = G_{2\text{m}}(z) - G_{60\text{m}}(z)$ (Hanks 2000; Sare & Hilley AGU 2015 T31A-2838).
  • Scarplet Template Matching (Sare & Hilley, JOSS 2018, doi:10.21105/joss.01066): $$W(x_r, y_r; \kappa t, \alpha) = -\frac{x_r}{2(\kappa t)^{3/2}\sqrt{\pi}}\exp!\left(-\frac{x_r^2}{4\kappa t}\right), \quad |x_r| \le 2\sqrt{\kappa t}\,\text{erfinv}(0.90), \;|y_r| \le \frac{L}{2}$$ with exact least-squares signal energy $T_1 = \text{xcorr}^2 / \sum W^2$, residual variance $\sigma_{\text{err}}^2 = \frac{1}{n}\max(T_3 - T_1, 0) + \epsilon$, $\text{SNR} = T_1 / \sigma_{\text{err}}^2$, and slope-aware contour alignment weight $w_{\text{slope}} = \frac{1}{2}(1 + |\cos(\alpha - \theta_{\text{reg}})|)$.
  • Steger (1998) Sub-Pixel Curvilinear Lines & Frangi (1998) Multiscale Hessian: Evaluated at $\sigma \in {3, 6, 12, 20}\text{ m}$ on $z_{\text{det}}$, solving $t = -(g_x n_x + g_y n_y)/\lambda_1$ with $|t n_x|\le 0.5, |t n_y|\le 0.5$ and $\mathcal{V}(\sigma) = \exp(-R_B^2/2\beta^2)(1 - \exp(-S^2/2c^2))$.
  • 100 m Aggregation: Aggregated $3337\times 3337$ native 3 m pixels into $101\times 101$ ($10,201$) 100 m competition grid cells per tile:
