Review findings — lidar-scarp lane, run 64

Decision

NEGATIVE / NOT CLEARED FOR SUBMISSION. One candidate TIFF was generated, hashed, validated and published for download labelled as not-cleared. No weekly slot was used, no promotion is claimed, and the owner-reported champion is not beaten on the evidence that this run was able to measure where the terrain-class mimic cannot explain the gain.

Every number below carries its evidence class:

  • PROXY-INSTRUMENT (sgmc_off) — a state-geological-map fault population located more than 300 m from the mapped catalogue, evaluated on the shared evaluator (gems57-pooled-hide-v3-se). It is not the hidden panel and it favours rock-exposed terrain.
  • HOLDOUT-DTI (catalogue_hide) — whole-component hide-and-recover on the mapped catalogue, shared evaluator, 4 label-blind quadrant folds, 80 px buffer.
  • REGISTRY-MEASUREMENT — byte, pixel, rank and dot-overlap comparisons against the reachable registry rasters.
  • ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED — none exists in this repository.

What was actually built

A catalogue-independent surface: the lane never uses distance-to-catalogue, catalogue density or any catalogue-derived feature to rank cells. The pipeline is 12 quantised 2 m lidar scarp channels and 7 quantised 3 m scarp channels, decoded from their published manifests, reduced to named components at 100 m (amplitude, SNR-weighted amplitude, step, crest/base curvature, vergence asymmetry, cross-strike consistency, coherence, relief, 3 m replication, coverage), then percentile-ranked inside an eligible domain (lidar-covered cells, at least 3 px from the mapped catalogue, outside the cultural mask) and thinned by a greedy 1.5 px blue-noise selection.

The run also ablated every component and the pre-declared equal-weight composite (0.45 amplitude / 0.30 coherence / 0.25 3 m replication) at 24 000 dots each (PROXY-INSTRUMENT (sgmc_off)). The composite is the worst arm measured (DTI 0.02372, credit 0.0540 per dot), because coherence (0.03217 / 0.0733) and the 3 m replication gate (0.02390 / 0.0544) carry almost nothing and the gate even anti-correlates with the state-map population (canary AUC 0.343). The adopted surface is therefore the strongest single component, amp_snr = sqrt(ex_max · ex_mean) (0.10352 / 0.2377), with amp_mean (0.10291), base (0.10263) and step (0.10252) statistically beside it; relief reaches 0.08366 and vergence, coh, replicate3m stay at or below 0.05. That is a selection made on the instrument, and every receipt says so.

Two things kill the discovery claim on this instrument.

  1. The gain is concentrated at the range front. 117 of 323 blocks (36 %) are classified range-front (they contain catalogue cells in the top relief decile) and hold 74 % of the instrument truth. Inside the 206 interior blocks the ordering inverts: champion 0.08767, candidate 0.01433 — a paired difference of −0.07333 (SE 0.01060), i.e. the champion beats the candidate by roughly 3.5 SE where range-front incision cannot earn the credit. Instrument 1 also rewards mass monotonically (0.23246 at 160 k dots), which is the signature of a terrain-class proxy rather than of a fault detector.
  2. A stricter truth changes nothing. Using only truth cells more than 800 m from the mapped catalogue (51 742 px), the pooled numbers barely move (candidate 0.13635, champion 0.08481) and the interior ordering is identical (champion 0.08140, candidate 0.01541, terrain-matched control 0.02135).

The internal control comparisons are equally informative: a fixed sibling artifact (the champion), which no arm of this run can influence, beats the candidate inside the interior stratum; and the terrain-matched control is statistically indistinguishable from the candidate. On this instrument the lane surface is, at best, a range-front relief detector.

Registry comparison — REGISTRY-MEASUREMENT

62 single-band rasters on the pinned grid were decoded and compared (bytes, decoded pixels, rank correlation, dot overlap, dot-set Jaccard).

  • byte_unique true, pixel_unique true — no prior raster shares the candidate's bytes or its decoded positive support.
  • Two comparators saturate the footprint: calib_r13lattice_00904.tif and r13_lattice_s5_00904.tif (206 895 dots each) place a 3 px neighbourhood over 99.87 % of the footprint, so every nonempty candidate exceeds the literal "≤ 70 % within 3 px" clause against them (IR-H71-UNIQ-01).
  • With comparators of near-total halo coverage set aside, the discriminating reading still fails: 88.8 % of the candidate's dots lie within 3 px of gems32-bayesopt-dilation-scarp-d28-20261004-* (45 000 dots) and 95.3 % within 3 px of r11_greedy_mp.tif (272 853 dots), with only 6.1 % / 0.9 % exact-dot Jaccard. The lane's top cells are the same physical scarp cells that earlier submissions already mined; the candidate is a fresh dot set on a heavily used surface. Verdict: negative (logged as a near-duplicate, not exempted).

Why the owner-reported champion scores highest

This run's measurements add to the sibling reconstruction (its support is reproduced by pruning, 2 545 deletions and zero additions):

  • On the honest-like population the champion is not a strong fault detector: PROXY-INSTRUMENT (sgmc_off) 0.09539 at 37 654 dots versus 0.13689 for the lane candidate; HOLDOUT-DTI (catalogue_hide) 0.00670.
  • Where it is strong is the interior stratum of the state-map population (0.08767 with the same dot budget, 6× the candidate) and the 800 m strict truth (0.08140). Its geometry therefore earns credit in low-relief, interior terrain — exactly the population where a lidar-visible alluvial scarp detector should earn it and where this run's surface does not.
  • The plausible explanation, consistent with every measurement here, is that the champion's advantage comes from placement across a broad, interior, non-halo field rather than from a stronger per-pixel detector: it is spread out (median 1.97 km from the mapped network in the sibling reconstruction, maximum 21.9 km), it is not confined to range fronts, and it is not a duplicate of the scarp-feature submissions that saturate the registry's overlap clauses.
  • Is a higher-scoring submission achievable? The evidence here says yes in principle and not from this lane: the field must earn credit in the interior stratum, must beat a terrain-matched control there by more than 2 SE, and must not be a near-duplicate of the scarp-feature family. No arm of this run does the first two, so no promotion is offered. The published candidate is a format-valid, hash-verifiable, byte-unique artifact with a measured pooled edge over the champion on one proxy instrument — and a measured 3.5 SE deficit in the stratum that decides the verdict.
