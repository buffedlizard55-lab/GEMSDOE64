What this file is

Hypothesis: Native-resolution lidar scarp evidence (scarplet-style template matching plus Steger/Frangi line measures) with the lane's strike-line coherence statistic recovers faults that the 100 m stack discards.

Mechanism: Slope-detrended scarp template fits give amplitude/SNR/orientation; sub-pixel Steger ridge/valley strength and multiscale Frangi vesselness localise the scarp line; the strike-line consistency statistic (fraction of aligned neighbours with evidence) is used as a keep-fraction veto on the packed dots; dots are spaced at 2.8-3.4 px and pruned on the visible catalogue flank.

Named non-fault process that could mimic it: Differential erosion or deposition along a lithologic contact / alluvial-fan edge produces a strike-coherent, slope-detrended step with no fault slip (and can survive the strike-line veto). Erosion, deposition and lithologic steps produce strike-coherent, slope-detrended scarps without fault slip; the strike-coherence veto reduces but does not eliminate that failure mode.

The uniqueness decision, spelled out

Two different verdicts are computed from the same measured scan, and both are published:

Why the literal gate cannot pass. The registry checked here contains one prior that carries positive mass at 100.0 % of the footprint cells (witness_17_E-proba-multiscale.tif). For such a prior the 3-pixel forward overlap of any non-empty candidate is ~1.0: the matched random controls in the same emission domain all reach 1.000 forward overlap. That is a property of the rule meeting a dense surface, not evidence about this file. The certificate therefore reports both numbers, takes no exemption, and leaves the literal gate failing — exactly as the sibling repositories' reviews do (their published review records the same 1.0 overlap and the same "NOT CLEARED" clearance).
