Irregularities

What does not add up — flagged, not smoothed

  1. Two different “current high scores” in the task brief. The brief quotes the GEMSDOE site at 0.3774 and later says 0.3195 is the highest. A leaderboard snapshot verified 2026-10-11 (rows read from the public leaderboard and stored in docs/data/leaderboard_snapshot.json) shows #1 = 0.4133, #8 = 0.3195. Treat any site-local “high score” as stale unless it carries a date and a row.
  2. 0.2778 vs 0.2747 for the same file. The h33 repository's own README marks that artifact UNSCORED with a projected live value of 0.2747; the brief reports 0.2778. Both are owner-reported. No organizer receipt exists in any repository checked; the discrepancy is preserved here rather than averaged.
  3. The dense prior and the literal uniqueness rule. A pinned prior in the registry (the 17GEMSDOE probability raster, sha256 ab0a0a62…, re-downloaded and hash-verified here) carries positive mass at essentially every cell of the footprint. The sibling protocol defines dots as “finite positive pixels”, so the 3-pixel forward-overlap statistic against that prior is ~1.0 for any non-empty candidate. That is a rule-meets-dense-prior degeneracy, not evidence about this candidate. It is reported, the literal verdict is left failing, and no exemption is taken.
  4. Egress limitation, honestly stated. The lane requires 1 m DEM processing. The sandbox can reach GitHub, PyPI and npm only; prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com, portal.opentopography.org, www.sciencebase.gov, data.source.coop and Dropbox all fail to resolve or connect (tested 2026-10-11). The published candidate therefore runs the same lane code on the finest reachable lidar-derived grid: the owner-CI 100 m aggregate of 706 USGS 3DEP 1 m tiles. The native-resolution path is implemented and unit-tested (tests/test_lidar_scarp.py), and switches on the moment a 1 m DEM is placed at --dem.
  5. Surrogate truth everywhere. No publication here contains a hidden-test score. Where a number looks like a score, the label says HOLDOUT-DTI (surrogate), MEASURED (bytes), or OWNER-REPORTED. Anything else would be a hallucination.
