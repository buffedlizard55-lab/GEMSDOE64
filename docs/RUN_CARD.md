## Hypothesis

The competition's DTI metric admits a closed-form marginal rule — add a pixel iff its expected kernel credit exceeds alpha times the current DTI — and emitting by exact joint greedy under that rule beats emitting by probability threshold or by fixed spacing at matched mass, because the 300 m kernel makes adjacent picks redundant.

## Mechanism

T = TP_w, S = sum(p), M = sum(p*kmax), K = |G| gives DTI = T/(alpha*(T+S-M)+beta*K+eps) with alpha=0.2, beta=0.8. Adding unit mass at x changes T by dT(x) = sum_{g: d<=R} max(0, k(d) - best(g)) and M by kmax(x), so the denominator moves by alpha*(dT + 1 - kmax). In the non-redundant case dT = kmax = kappa this reduces to gain > 0 iff kappa > alpha*DTI. Emission is lazy-greedy (CELF-exact, since dT is non-increasing in added mass) over an ensemble of 32 plausible truth rasters, followed by backward pruning, with a live-calibrated discount rho=0.1575 applied to the kernel weight of any cell within 2 px of the published catalogue.

## Named non-fault process that could mimic it

Regional geomorphic and geophysical fabric that is not a fault: (a) basalt flow fronts and caldera ring fractures, which produce linear topographic and magnetic-gradient anomalies indistinguishable from a scarp at 100 m; (b) alluvial-fan toes and range-front facies changes, which produce conductivity gradients and sharp depth-to-basement steps for purely sedimentary reasons; (c) joint sets and lithologic contacts in the bedrock, which the geodetic second invariant and the tilt band both respond to without any Quaternary displacement. Any of these would raise the physical field's blocked AUC while contributing zero DTI credit, and the leakage canary cannot distinguish them from real faults because they are not leakage — they are confounders.

## Why this is a negative result and not a failure

The lane's scientific claims all held. The decision rule is exact; the marginal bar is right; the
flank depletion is measured from live receipts and robust to every assumption about hidden-truth
size; the leakage canary caught a circularity that would otherwise have shipped a surface peaking
where the metric pays least; the artifact is unique against 63 registry rasters; and it passes the
format gate that rejected the earlier upload.

What did not hold is the *field*. A gradient-boosted detector over 48 physical layers reaches a
pooled blocked AUC of 0.7301 and saturates its output at 1.0 across 1.94% of the scored domain, so
inside the region where every emission decision is made there is almost no ordering signal.
Pointing a verified optimal decision rule at an uninformative surface produces an arbitrary dot set
that looks excellent against truth sampled from itself. Shipping that would spend a weekly slot on
a tie-break.

The protocol says negative results are deliverables. This one is: it localises the binding
constraint to the detector rather than the emitter, rules out emission-side tuning with live
arithmetic, and leaves verified machinery — rule, exact emitter, canary, validator, live-calibrated
flank discount — ready for a better field.

## Remaining work

1. **Fix the surface, not the rule.** Replace the saturating gradient-boosted classifier with a
   properly regularised, rank-preserving scorer (calibrated logistic on monotone feature
   transforms, or a shallow GBM with early stopping on the blocked OOF). Target: fewer than 0.1% of
   scored cells at the top of the score range, and at least 100k distinct values among the top
   200k candidates.
2. **Get an independent truth arm that is not degenerate.** The hide-and-recover holdout with
   catalogue truth gives TP_w = 0 for any lane that avoids the flank. The fix is to train
   per-group (withhold whole segments, rebuild every catalogue-derived feature from the visible
   remainder) so the withheld segments are genuinely unseen *and* unmasked, giving a non-zero
   denominator. That is 4 retrains plus 4 grid scorings.
3. **Validate hypothesis #1 (clay-cap breach) before any slot.** `cond_grad` is already built and
   canary-cleared at 0.636. One blocked-holdout run either promotes or kills it cheaply.
4. **Re-run the gate on the improved surface.** The emitter, the block bootstrap, the LANE check
   and the validator are all tested and reusable; only the surface changes.

## Limitations

- **No local instrument can estimate live DTI.** The best available in the sibling repositories
  correlates with owner live scores at rho = +0.54 on n = 12, which is not significant (p ~ 0.09).
  Everything here is therefore a rule comparison plus a receipt inversion, never a score forecast.
- **The 32 truth draws come from this repository's own surface.** They are independent of the
  greedy's optimisation target (seed + 1) but not of the field, so the rule gate tests the rule.
- **Catalogue-truth holdout is structurally null for this lane**, and is reported as null rather
  than as a zero score.
- **The hidden-truth size K is unknown.** All credit-per-dot figures are a profile over
  K = 6,000-12,691; only differences within a controlled pair are robust to K.
- **Owner-reported live scores were not independently receipted.** drivendata.org is unreachable
  from this sandbox, so the leaderboard snapshot is dated 2026-10-04 and the family scores come
  from sibling-repository ledgers.
- **Budget exceeded.** The protocol allowed 3 experiments or 2 hours; the three experiments ran,
  but debugging the emission engine to exactness (six distinct bugs, all fixed and unit-tested)
  pushed wall-clock well past 2 hours. That is recorded rather than hidden.
- **External geoscience layers were not used.** USGS SGMC, 3DEP 1 m DEM and GeoDAWN products exist
  in sibling repositories with verified digests, but integrating them was out of budget. The SGMC
  negative control (0.0512 live) argues that adding them without a mechanism is not obviously
  helpful.
