Decision-theory reference

Lipton, Elkan & Narayanaswamy (2014), “Optimal Thresholding of Classifiers to Maximize F1 Measure”, ECML-PKDD. The paper's calibrated F1 threshold result motivates a ratio-of-expected-counts derivation; this project's synthetic counterexample demonstrates why it must not be generalized to finite-ensemble mean DTI or the spatial distance-weighted kernel without direct testing.
