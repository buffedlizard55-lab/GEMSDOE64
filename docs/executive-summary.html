  2. The terrain-matched control is not beaten.

    Dots drawn from the same relief × slope classes score 0.12407 against the candidate's 0.13689 (candidate minus control: +0.01282, SE 0.00722).

  3. Instrument 2 shows no harm, and no edge.

    The published artifact scored as-is returns 0.00000 on all four folds by construction (its dots are ≥ 3 px from the whole catalogue while this instrument's truth is catalogue geometry) — a structural diagnostic, not a harm measurement. The protocol construction (per-fold placement from the visible catalogue) scores 0.06183 (0.04957–0.07344) against the mass-matched random control 0.07548 and the terrain-matched control 0.06299: statistically tied.
