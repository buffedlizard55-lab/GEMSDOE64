Decision rule, verified before use (E1)

On synthetic rasters the marginal dot is accepted exactly when its calibrated kernel credit clears alpha × DTI. At alpha = 0.2 and DTI = 0.30 the bar is 0.06; the measured switch is 0.0610 (analytic 0.0600), monotone in kappa.

Lipton/Elkan/Narayanaswamy generalisation: at alpha = 0.5 the optimal independent threshold is 0.0835 vs the predicted F1*/2 = 0.0904 (rel. error 8.2%); at alpha = 0.2, 0.0560 vs 0.0533.

Greedy add + backward prune reached the exhaustive optimum on all 3 synthetic rasters (gap 0.0 over 8,192 enumerated subsets each), and the mean-field expected DTI agrees with a 64-draw plausible-truth Monte Carlo within 1.6 SE.
